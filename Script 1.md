# Revenue Model

You can diversify income beyond commissions:

- **Campaign commission:** 10–20% of every completed campaign.
- **Subscription plans:** Premium features for businesses and advertisers.
- **Featured listings:** Businesses pay to appear first in search results.
- **Priority campaign placement:** Urgent campaigns get promoted.
- **Verified badges:** Optional paid verification after identity checks.
- **Content creation services:** Offer design or copywriting packages through freelancers on the platform.
- **API integrations:** Charge enterprise customers for CRM or marketing integrations.
- **Escrow fee:** Hold campaign funds until proof of posting is approved, increasing trust.

# Advanced Features to Differentiate Your Platform

To stand out from simple advertising marketplaces, consider these capabilities:

- **Audience Quality Score:** Rank advertisers using completion history, average engagement, and business ratings rather than only contact count.
- **Geo-targeted campaigns:** Businesses can target specific counties, towns, or neighborhoods.
- **Campaign scheduling:** Advertisers receive reminders and posting windows (e.g., 8–10 AM and 6–9 PM).
- **Multi-advertiser campaigns:** A single campaign can automatically recruit hundreds of advertisers based on filters.
- **Digital contracts:** Both parties accept campaign terms before work begins.
- **Dispute resolution center:** Structured workflow for handling disagreements over proof, timing, or payment.
- **Promo code tracking:** Each advertiser gets a unique code or landing page to measure conversions.
- **QR code generation:** Businesses can include QR codes in promotional creatives for easier tracking.
- **Campaign templates:** Save reusable campaign setups for recurring promotions.
- **API-ready architecture:** Allow future integration with accounting systems, CRMs, and marketing tools.

## Suggested Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** React or Next.js with TypeScript
- **Backend:** Laravel (PHP) or Node.js (NestJS)
- **Database:** PostgreSQL for transactional data
- **Cache & Queues:** Redis
- **Storage:** S3-compatible object storage for media uploads
- **Authentication:** JWT/OAuth with optional two-factor authentication
- **Payments:** M-Pesa (Daraja API), Airtel Money, PayPal, Stripe (for international users)
- **Notifications:** Email, SMS, and WhatsApp Business API
- **Analytics:** Dashboard with charts, campaign KPIs, and exportable reports

This concept has the potential to become a specialized influencer marketing platform for WhatsApp, especially in African markets where WhatsApp is one of the primary communication channels. The key differentiator will be **trust**—through verified advertisers, escrow-based payments, measurable campaign performance, and transparent ratings—so that businesses feel confident investing their advertising budgets.
